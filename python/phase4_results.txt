BUSINESS INSIGHTS
Transaction data:
The cleaned dataset contains transaction-level retail records with customer, date, product, quantity, price, and revenue information.

Revenue distribution:
Transaction-level revenue is highly right-skewed. Most transactions generate relatively low revenue, while a small number have substantially higher values, 
creating a long right tail. This indicates that a relatively small number of high-value transactions contribute significantly to revenue.

Revenue over time:
Monthly revenue shows substantial variation across the dataset period, with pronounced peaks in late 2010 and late 2011. 
Revenue rises strongly around September–November. December 2011 should be interpreted cautiously because the dataset ends on December 9, 
making it a partial month.

Top customers:
Customer 18102 is the highest revenue-generating customer, followed by 14646 and 14156. 
Other high-value customers include 14911, 17450, 13694, 17511, 16446, 16684, and 12415. 
This shows that revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of high-value customers.

Patterns and business opportunities:
The main patterns are strong right-skewness in transaction revenue, substantial monthly revenue variation, and concentration of revenue among high-value customers. 
These customers are important targets for retention and personalized marketing. 
High-value transactions should also be investigated to determine whether they are driven by bulk purchases, expensive products, or unusual purchasing behaviour.
